Interview Dos and Don'ts — Legal Guidelines

Some common questions asked during the interview process may be illegal. Interviewers should only ask questions that provide information about the person's ability to do the job.

Avoid questions related to these areas:

Age / Date of BirthDisabilityEthnicity Sex / Sexual OrientationCitizenshipMaiden Name PregnancyPlanning on Having ChildrenNumber of Children Childcare ArrangementsMarital StatusCurrent/Past Salary Credit HistoryHome OwnershipAuto Ownership Lowest Salary AcceptanceCriminal HistoryUnion Activity

How to Use This Guide
  • Ask each candidate the same questions per competency.
  • Take notes on concrete examples using STAR: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
  • Rate each response on the 1–5 scale after the interview.
  • Ask no more than 1 question from each competency section.
  • Time constraints may prevent exploring every competency — that is acceptable.
